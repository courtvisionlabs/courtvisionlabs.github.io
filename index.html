About

Built from inside the badminton community

CourtVision was created after seeing how player videos, coaching feedback, training goals, and progress records often remain disconnected.

We are building badminton-specific technology that helps athletes understand their development, and helps coaches extend their guidance between lessons.

Our team combines applied-science leadership, computer vision, product development, tournament production, and direct experience supporting junior badminton players and families.
