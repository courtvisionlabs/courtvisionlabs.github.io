Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email courtvisionlab@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. If something isn't working, tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, what you did, and what happened — a screenshot helps.

Setting up the phone for a drill

Stand the phone beside the net, just off the court on the player's left (their left as they face the net), at about eye level, in landscape, pointing back toward the baseline. Then drag the four dots to the corners of the area from the short service line to the baseline. The app's Phone Setup guide shows this with a diagram.

"The AI Coach is getting a lot of requests"

The AI analysis runs on a free service with a daily limit shared by everyone. Your drill is saved — try again in a few minutes, or later in the day.

Livestreaming to YouTube

Connect your YouTube account from the person icon → YouTube Account. Your channel needs live streaming turned on, which YouTube can take up to 24 hours to enable the first time.

Keeping score from another phone

On the match camera screen, tap Remote Scorer and have someone scan the QR code with their phone's camera — no app needed. Only share the code with your scorer: anyone with it can change that match's score until it ends.

Apple Watch

Install CourtVision on your Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone (Available Apps). You can keep score, and start or stop drill recordings, from your wrist.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap the person icon at the top right, then Delete Account, and confirm with Apple. This deletes your account and everything we store for it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
